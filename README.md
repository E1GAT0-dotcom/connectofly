# Connectofly

A digitized fruit fly nervous system running in Scratch blocks, with a 3D fly you can experiment on.

Play: https://turbowarp.org/?project_url=e1gat0-dotcom.github.io/connectofly/connectofly.sb3

The file is big and needs a strong computer. It takes a while to load, and runs best after a couple of minutes.

## Credits

- Brain: FlyWire (Princeton), Dorkenwald et al. and Schlegel et al., Nature 2024. Data licence CC BY-NC 4.0: free and non-commercial.
- Neuron model: Shiu et al., Nature 2024.
- Nerve cord: BANC, Bates et al., Nature 2026 (CC BY 4.0).

A fan project, not made by FlyWire. 54 antenna-lobe local neurons were changed to inhibitory to stop runaway firing, so the wiring differs from the published model. Hunger, thirst, the walking rhythm and some reactions to wind, water and mould are hand-built, not from the wiring.
